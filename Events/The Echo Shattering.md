# Background

The Echo Shattering — also known as Ragnarök — was the defining cataclysm of the ancient world. It occurred during a period when four great powers dominated the known world: the Jinni Transcendent States, the Dark Councils of the Spider People, the [[Elves|Elven]] City-States, and the [[Lizardfolk]] Communion Territories. Their combined magical activity, divine interventions, and the escalating conflicts between rival factions of the Aethani pantheon destabilized the [[The Echo Plane|Echo Plane]] itself.

# Description

The Great Sundering was a massive conflict that took place *within* the Echo Plane itself. This conflict tore holes between dimensions, causing catastrophic divine system failures across the entire world:

- **Jinni Floating Cities** crashed to earth, ending millennia of aerial civilization
- **Spider People's Web Networks** collapsed, shattering their psychic communication systems
- **Elven Divine Compacts** were broken, dissolving the patron deity relationships that had sustained their city-states
- **The Long Winter:** Climate disruption devastated global civilization
- **[[Lizardfolk]] Spawning Circles** and communal nature spirits were disrupted

The Shattering proved that the stability of civilizations built on Echo magic was directly dependent on the stability of the Echo Plane itself. When the metaphysical foundation cracked, every superstructure built upon it collapsed simultaneously.

# Aftermath

## Birth of the Phoenix (-5450)
In the heart of this chaos, the [[Phoenix Emperor]] was born — a being with unprecedented psychic architecture spanning multiple planes. His emergence may have been reality's response to the overwhelming chaos.

## The Phoenix Ascension (-5300 to -5200)
The exhausted Aethani gods submitted to the Phoenix Emperor's authority (the Exhausted Submission, -5280), leading to the creation of the Divine Veil System (-5270) and the unification of the elven city-states into the [[Phoenix Empire]] (-5260 to -5200).

## Long-term Consequences
The Echo Shattering fundamentally reset civilization. The ancient world of floating cities, psychic networks, and freely-intervening gods was replaced by the Phoenix Emperor's ordered system of constrained deities and centralized power. The memory of the Shattering served as both trauma and justification — the chaos it caused was the Phoenix's primary argument for why divine authority needed to be controlled.
