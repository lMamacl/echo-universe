# Background

The War of Two Empires was the final struggle for Phoenix succession, triggered by the complete fragmentation of the [[Phoenix Emperor]]'s consciousness (-3050) and the subsequent collapse of the divine binding system. Two rival factions emerged to claim the imperial legacy:

- **Orthodox Faction:** [[Empress Aetheria]] the Restorer, backed by Phoenix homeland nobility, claiming legitimate succession through traditional bloodlines.
- **Revolutionary Faction:** [[Khaine]]'s Avatar, a charismatic [[Dark Elves|Dark Elf]] war-leader chosen as the War-God's divine champion, claiming the empire through right of divine strength.

# Description

## The Rival Claims (-2950 to -2940)
The conflict crystallized around a fundamental question: should power derive from bloodline legitimacy or divine martial mandate? Both sides gathered supporters from their respective spheres of influence.

## The Great Campaigns (-2940 to -2890)
- **Western Offensive (-2940 to -2930):** Aetheria's forces attempted to reclaim rebellious eastern territories
- **Naval Wars (-2935 to -2920):** [[Dark Elves|Dark Elf]] maritime supremacy disrupted imperial supply lines
- **Continental Campaign (-2925 to -2915):** Khaine's Avatar led massive land invasion of the Phoenix heartland
- **Siege of the Golden City (-2920 to -2910):** Year-long siege of the Phoenix capital became a symbol of imperial collapse
- **Population Devastation (-2920 to -2890):** Millions died in increasingly desperate warfare

## The Cataclysmic End (-2890 to -2886)
- **The Forbidden Ritual (-2888):** [[Empress Aetheria]] attempted to bind new divine power through dark magic
- **Magical Catastrophe (-2887):** Uncontrolled forces destroyed both armies, killing Aetheria
- **Khaine's Avatar Triumphant (-2887):** War-god's champion survived, claimed the Phoenix throne
- **The Blood Months (-2887 to -2886):** Brief reign of terror and violence

## Divine Intervention (-2886)
- [[The Spider God|The Secret God]] manifested, killed Khaine's Avatar, and bound the war-god to a prison realm
- With no legitimate authority remaining, the [[Phoenix Empire]] formally ceased to exist

# Aftermath

The War of Two Empires ushered in the Dark Ages — 800 years of fragmentation, warfare, and gradual recovery:
- Former imperial territories splintered into the Hundred Kingdoms
- Trade networks and administrative systems collapsed
- Technological regression as imperial knowledge-sharing systems were lost
- Eventually, a charismatic warlord claiming descent from the last Phoenix Empress would found the [[Second Empire]], marking Year Zero of the new calendar
