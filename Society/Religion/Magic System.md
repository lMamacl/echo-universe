# Magic System

> [!important] Zasada fundamentalna
> Magia w Echo Universe nie pochodzi z many, czarów czy sił natury. Jest to akt **manifestacji** — urzeczywistnienie konceptu istniejącego w [[The Echo Plane|Płaszczyźnie Echa]] w świecie materialnym.

## Core Principle: Fuel, Knowledge, and Will

Manifestation is not free; it requires a combination of energy (fuel), psychological focus (will), and cognitive design (knowledge).

### 1. The Fuel (Source of Energy)
Mages do not generate magical energy from nothing. They draw it from living sources:
- **Vital Force:** The mage's own life force and stamina. Drawing too much causes physical exhaustion, rapid aging, or organ failure.
- **Environment:** Energy can be siphoned from surrounding organic life (plants, trees), leaving localized areas of blight.
- **Echo Resonators:** Natural points of puncture where the barrier between worlds is thin, offering massive reservoirs of Echo energy.

### 2. The Mental Architecture
- **Will/Emotion (Primal Manifestation):** Emotional magic is fast, violent, and dangerous. Primal feelings (fury, terror, desperation) shape the Echo. Without structure, it is highly volatile. An emotional attempt to heal might mend a wound but cause cancerous growths or physical deformation due to lack of control.
- **Knowledge/Plan (Precise Manifestation):** Complex acts require detailed cognitive plans. A healer must understand anatomy; a builder must comprehend geometry and architecture. Magic acts as the tool that realizes the blueprint, not the blueprint itself.

### 3. Echo Corruption (Ethereal Fade)
Every interaction with the Echo leaves a mark on the soul and body. Over-reliance on manifestation causes **Echo Bleed**:
- The mage’s body and mind slowly align with the frequencies of the Immaterium.
- **Physical Symptoms:** Loss of touch and physical sensation, progressive transparency of flesh (ethereal fading), and physical coldness.
- **Mental Symptoms:** Hearing whispers, experiencing hallucinations/visions of the Echo Plane, and a feeling of profound alienation from the physical world.
- Eventually, the mage fades entirely, their consciousness absorbed by the Echo while their physical form dissolves.

## Daily Practice and Education

Magical talent is a rare spectrum of sensitivity to the Echo Plane:
- **Minor Gift (2.5% of population):** Individuals capable of minor manifestations. They work as hedge-wizards, local healers, priests, or craftsmen using simple Echo-runes to reinforce armor and tools.
- **Refined Gift (0.075% of population):** True mages capable of complex, high-level manifestations. They undergo rigorous training.

### Practical Casting
Magical acts are structured through:
- **Meditation:** Quieting the mind to reach the Echo Plane.
- **Incantations:** Verbal keys that help focus the mage's mental architecture.
- **Runes:** Geometric blueprints that anchor Echo energy to physical objects (essential for enchanting and crafting).

### Institutions of Learning
1. **Academies (Northern Republics):** Treat magic as a progressive science, focusing on runic engineering and standardized spells.
2. **Czeladnictwo (Apprenticeships):** Guilds of craftsmen passing down secret family runes and specialized techniques.
3. **Priesthood (Second Empire):** Treat magic as divine grace, wrapping manifestation in liturgical rites.

## The Two Schools of Dark Magic

"Dark Magic" is defined by its transgressive methods and the threat it poses to the fabric of reality:

### 1. Transmutation (Flesh Weaving)
The manipulation of living or dead flesh. Rather than manifest inanimate concepts, these practices overwrite biological code:
- **Necromancy:** Reanimating dead matter.
- **Dragon-Birth Ritual:** Fusing human souls with beast flesh.
- **Creation of Orks (Zmienieni):** Forcefully morphing human DNA with beast templates.
- **Lamarckian Genetics:** These changes permanently modify the genetic line, making acquired magical mutations hereditary.

### 2. Echo Manipulation (Summoning)
Directly tearing the barrier between worlds to pull raw Echo entities (demons, spectres) or raw Echo energy into the material world. It requires no physical blueprint, only raw emotional torment or blood sacrifice to bridge the gap.

## Deep Lore (The Law of Conservation)
*Unknown to the general populace:*
- **Spectra** are a finite resource in the Echo Plane, though tied to the total psychic output of living beings.
- Every magical manifestation draws the material world and the Echo closer together, slowly wearing down the Veil. Whether this convergence will ultimately fuse the worlds or destroy the material plane remains a profound, unanswered mystery.

---

## Inheritance and Specialization

- **Genetic Compatibility:** Inter-fertility exists between all human subspecies, but compatibility varies. Human-Elf crosses are highly compatible, producing half-elves who have played significant historical roles.
- **Magical Affinity:** Elves exhibit a higher affinity for refined magical talent (yielding more high-tier mages), though their overall occurrence rate of the minor gift remains close to the global 2.5% average.
