# Description

Elves are the dominant civilization of the Echo Universe's core history. They are not a separate species but a **magically-evolved branch of humanity** — descendants of *Homo sapiens* who underwent generations of magical transmutation, resulting in deep and lasting ethnic differences including physical traits, physiological adaptations, and innate magical affinities.

> [!important] Kluczowa zasada
> All "races" in the Echo Universe (Elves, [[Lizardfolk]], Jinni-descendants, Spider People) are **inter-fertile human subspecies**. Their differences stem from generations of Echo-powered transmutation, not separate biological origins.

## Biology and Lifespan
- **Lifespan:** Elves possess a significantly slower aging process compared to unmodified humans. An average Elf lives to **140–150 years**, with some reaching up to **180 years** of age.
- **Genetic Compatibility:** While inter-fertility exists between all human branches, the genetic link between Core Humans and Elves is exceptionally strong and compatible.
- **Half-Elves (Półelfy):** Cross-unions are common and highly viable, producing half-elves who inherit elements of elven longevity and human adaptability. Half-elves have historically played critical roles as mediators, warlords, and scholars in both the First and Second Empires.

# History

## City-States and Patron Deities (-7500 to -6500)
Individual elven cities developed exclusive relationships with specific gods from the emerging pantheon — the Divine Compact. This led to spectacular architectural achievements, including temples that existed partially in the [[The Echo Plane|Echo Plane]].

## The Divine Schism (-6500 to -5500)
The elven pantheon fractured into Celestial (order, hierarchy) and Natural (wilderness, balance) factions. The Celestial faction's victory led to the [[Green Elves]] Exodus (-6100 to -6000) and the Purge of the Groves.

## Under the Phoenix (-5260 to -2886)
The [[Phoenix Emperor]] unified the elven city-states into the [[Phoenix Empire]]. Elves became the administrative and colonial class, spreading across the known world. Sub-groups emerged:
- **Core Elves:** Imperial heartland population.
- **[[Dark Elves]]:** Eastern island colonists who developed distinct identity.
- **[[Green Elves]]:** Nature-faction refugees and their descendants.
- **Colonial Elves:** Administrators in desert and lizardfolk territories.

## Post-Empire
After the [[Phoenix Empire]]'s dissolution, elven successor states dominated the Western Peninsula, eventually consolidating into the [[Second Empire]].

# Culture

Elven culture emphasizes urbanization, hierarchy, and divine authority. Their relationship with the Echo Plane is mediated through organized religion and institutional structures rather than the communal or nature-based approaches of other peoples.

# Other Notes

The term "Elf" in this universe functions more like an ethnic or cultural designation than a species name. Physically, elves differ from other human groups in ways produced by millennia of Echo-powered transmutation (such as longer lifespan, slender build, and heightened refined magic affinity).
