# About Misha

Misha is the Goddess of Nature, Pestilence, the Wild, and Abominations — the patron deity of the [[Green Elves]]. Unlike most Echo-Gods who were individual heroes who achieved [[Apotheosis and Divinity|apotheosis]], Misha is unique: she is the **collective mind of the priestesses of the Green Pact**, preserving the connection between the sacred groves.

This makes her a fundamentally different kind of deity — not a single transcended individual, but a merged consciousness of an entire priestly order. This collective nature likely makes her more stable than individual Echo-Gods (she doesn't depend on a single personality maintaining coherence), but also more alien and less predictable in her motivations.

# History

Misha's formation likely occurred during or after the Green Elves Exodus (-6100 to -6000), when the Natural faction of the elven pantheon was defeated by the Celestial faction. As the priestesses of the Green Pact fled the Purge of the Groves, their collective consciousness may have crystallized into a single divine entity within the Echo Plane — a desperate act of preservation that transformed mortal devotion into divine existence.

Her domains — Nature, but also Pestilence and Abominations — suggest a deity who embodies the wild, untamed, and sometimes horrifying aspects of the natural world. She is not a gentle goddess of gardens, but the raw force of nature in all its beautiful and terrifying manifestations.

# Appearance

Not described in the historical record. As a collective consciousness, Misha may not have a singular appearance — potentially manifesting as many-voiced, many-faced, or in forms that shift between the various priestesses whose minds compose her being.

# Abilities

As a collective-mind deity, Misha's powers likely include:
- Maintaining psychic connections between distant groves
- Nature manipulation on a vast scale
- Biological transformation (including pestilence and "abominations")
- Communion with all natural life within her domain

# Other Notes

The duality of Misha's domains — nature *and* abominations — reflects the Echo Universe's core philosophy that magic is a reflection of consciousness, not an inherently good or evil force. The wild is both life-giving and terrifying, creative and destructive.
