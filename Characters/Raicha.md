# About Raicha

Raicha is the Draconic Lich Queen — one of the most powerful entities to emerge from the Dragon-Birth Ritual. She represents the ultimate corruption of the original defensive purpose of the [[Lizardfolk]] transformation: a being who combined the consciousness of a mortal leader with the body of a prehistoric reptile and the dark magic of the Echo Plane, and then transcended even death.

# History

Raicha's origins lie in the [[Council of Scales]]' desperate gambit against [[Phoenix Empire]] expansion. When the Lizardfolk shamans performed the Dragon-Birth Ritual in -4765, council leaders fused their souls with wyvern flesh through dark magical processes. While these first dragons successfully halted the Phoenix advance, the dark magic that created them carried its own psychological imperatives — whispering promises of dominion that grew stronger with each passing season.

Raicha appears to have embraced the corruption more fully than most, eventually achieving a form of undeath that made her a lich-queen among dragons — an abomination sustained by Echo power and dark magic alike.

# Appearance

As a Draconic Lich, Raicha likely presents a terrifying fusion of draconic power and undeath — a being whose physical form reflects both the prehistoric might of her reptilian body and the corruption of the dark magic that sustains her existence beyond natural death.

# Abilities

- Draconic power (aerial supremacy, dragon-fire)
- Dark magic mastery
- Lich-like undeath and persistence
- Connection to the Echo Plane through corrupted channels

# Other Notes

Raicha rules from the Dragon Lands (Kraina Smoków), located west of the central mountain range on the Southern Continent. Her domain is described as home to "beings driven by pure hatred and dark magic" — failed gods who merged their consciousness in the Echo Plane with mutated prehistoric reptile bodies.
