# About The Spider God

The Spider God is a deity whose web entangled whole societies, binding them into one collective slave-mind. A god of secrets and hidden knowledge, this entity represents perhaps the most insidious form of Echo-power — the manipulation and binding of consciousness itself.

> [!warning] Identity Question
> The "Secret God" mentioned in the timeline (-2886) who killed Khaine's Avatar and bound the war-god to a prison realm may be the same entity. If so, this deity played a pivotal role in ending the War of Two Empires and the Phoenix Empire itself.

# History

## Origins in the Web Concordat
The Spider People's ancient arachnid civilization developed sophisticated magical democracy through the Web Concordat — interconnected city-states linked by both physical and psychic networks. Their mastery of illusion magic, biological manipulation, and collective consciousness techniques suggests the Spider God emerged from (or was worshipped by) this civilization.

## The Secret God's Intervention (-2886)
A "long-dormant deity of secrets and hidden knowledge" manifested at the end of the [[War of Two Empires]], killed [[Khaine]]'s Avatar during the Blood Months, and bound the war-god to a magical prison realm. This decisive act ended the final chapter of the [[Phoenix Empire]]'s history.

# Appearance

Unknown and likely unknowable. As a god of secrets whose nature involves hiding and binding, the Spider God's true form — if one exists — is probably never directly observed.
