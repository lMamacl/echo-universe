# Description

## About

The Southern Continent is the more exotic and dangerous of the two main landmasses, separated from the [[Northern Continent]] by the Inner Sea (Morze Wewnętrzne). It is the origin of some of the world's oldest civilizations and most powerful entities — including the birthplace of the [[Phoenix Emperor]] himself.

## Features

### Land of Two Rivers (Kraina Dwóch Rzek)
Located in the northeastern part of the continent, across the Inner Sea. An ancient civilization centered around two mighty rivers — the cradle of states ruled by descendants of the Ginni (Genie Lords), among the first primordial beings to achieve [[Apotheosis and Divinity|apotheosis]].

### Dragon Lands (Kraina Smoków)
Located west of the central mountain range. The domain of powerful beings known as dragons — beings born from the failed apotheosis of [[Lizardfolk]] leaders, who merged their consciousness in the Echo Plane with mutated prehistoric reptile bodies. Ruled by entities like [[Raicha]], the Draconic Lich Queen, these creatures are driven by hatred and dark magic.

### Isolated Empire (Izolowane Cesarstwo)
A powerful, isolationist state located east of the mountains in a subtropical zone. Its philosophy and social structure are based on the concept of the "Great Web" — the connection of all living beings through the [[The Echo Plane|Echo Plane]].

### Corsair Kingdoms (Królestwa Korsarzy)
Island kingdoms on the eastern coast, a constant threat to the Isolated Empire. Inhabited by descendants of rebellious followers of the god [[Khaine|Kayne]], who defied the [[Phoenix Emperor]]. Their culture is a dark mirror of the imperial traditions from the [[Great Western Isles]]. Dominated by piracy and the slave trade.

## Populations

- Jinni-descendants (Land of Two Rivers)
- [[Elves]] (Great Western Isles, colonial presence)
- Dragons and [[Lizardfolk]] (Dragon Lands)
- Inhabitants of the Isolated Empire (unique culture)
- [[Khaine|Kayne]]-worshipping corsairs (Corsair Kingdoms)

## Climate

Ranges from Mediterranean-equivalent (northern coasts) to subtropical (eastern regions) and arid (Dragon Lands western areas).

# History

The Southern Continent is the origin of some of the world's most ancient civilizations and the launching point of the [[Phoenix Empire]]'s global conquest. See [[World History Timeline]] for detailed chronology.

# Significant locations in Southern Continent

- Land of Two Rivers — ancient Genie-lord states
- [[Great Western Isles]] — birthplace of the [[Phoenix Emperor]]
- Dragon Lands — domain of [[Raicha]] and the corrupted dragons
- Isolated Empire — "Great Web" civilization
- Corsair Kingdoms — [[Khaine]] worship and piracy

# Other Notes

The Southern Continent represents the "other" in Echo Universe civilization — more mysterious, more dangerous, and home to powers that predate or rival the Northern Continent's elvish dominance.
